What we do

Two services. One connected brand.

ZENZORA is designed around two everyday needs: getting food delivered and booking a rider.

Food delivery

Customers can discover food options from participating vendors and request delivery through the ZENZORA ordering experience.

➤

Vendor & rider tools

The experience

Simple from request to fulfilment.

Our service model is designed to keep each participant's role clear.

01 / CUSTOMER

Choose a service

A customer requests food delivery or a rider service through the available ZENZORA channel.

02 / PARTNER

Vendor or rider responds

The relevant vendor or rider receives the request through their dedicated ZENZORA tools.

03 / DELIVERY

Service is completed

The order or ride is fulfilled, bringing the customer and service partner together.
